## Steam — About This Game

**The lawn wars are real.**

CALL OF KAREN: HOA WARFARE is a satirical 6v6 suburban tactical first-person
shooter. Pick your faction — from the Karens (the manager is already on line
two) to the HOA Dads (measuring your grass with a ruler), the VC Bros, the
Woke Libs, the Rednecks, the Gym Rats, and 14 more neighborhood archetypes —
and fight for control of the cul-de-sac.

**Every faction is fully voiced.** Over 1,100 hand-written voice lines cover
spawns, kills, headshots, streaks, victories, defeats, and faction-vs-faction
rivalries. The Karens do NOT get along with the Woke Libs. The Pickleball
Retirees have annexed the tennis courts. You will hear about it.

**Key Features**

- **20 playable suburban factions**, each with a distinct voice, personality,
  and full voice-line set — no silent protagonists here
- **3 game modes**: Cul-de-Sac Clash (team deathmatch), Receipt Check
  (kill-confirmed), and Evict the Block (objective)
- **Suburban arsenal**: the Allowance AR, Lawsuit Grenades, the Participation
  Trophy (melee), and more
- **Rivalry system**: matched factions trash-talk each other with bespoke
  kill lines — 28+ directional rivalries and counting
- **Camping snitch**: hide too long and your own team rats you out
- **Killcams with custom taunts**, kill streaks, headshot bonuses, and
  full match flow — all playable in the browser demo

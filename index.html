Rules of Engagement

Tactical Anti-Cheese Systems

Engine-enforced mechanics preventing suburban stagnation:

⏱️ 17s Camping Snitch
Loitering in a 3.1m radius triggers a 3-second flashing warning siren. At 20s, your character automatically barks a snitch taunt and is revealed on every opponent's radar for 6 seconds.
📡 Rotating Tactical Radar
Player-centric rotating HUD compass keeps forward vision UP. Highlights neighborhood road boundaries, suburban houses, active King of the Lawn zones, and gunfire blips for 1.8 seconds.
🔫 Sidearm Swap (Q / 1 / 2)
Quickly switch between primary rifles and the Permit sidearm. Ammo is banked per weapon slot, preventing lost reloads during rapid tactical exchanges.
🦘 Trampoline & Roof Cover
Center-court trampoline launches players onto car hoods and patio roof vantage points. Lawsuit grenades can bounce off cars, trampolines, and curbs.
